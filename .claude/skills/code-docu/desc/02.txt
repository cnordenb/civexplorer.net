@ js/view/stringbuilder.js
The page's text: the fixed texts, the texts of the chosen language, the list of civilisations and Filters, the loading
  screen, and the small helpers every view uses. Its views of the texts, the civilisations and Filters are parts of the
  page in page.js.
- $: Short for `document.getElementById`, used wherever the page is touched.
- clone: Copies the element inside a template in index.html. Repeated markup lives in index.html (Rule 2); the code only
  fills it in.
- simple: Tells whether Simple UI is on, from the `pictures` class on the page, which is what shows the pictures. One
  source of truth, readable from every script.
- place: Tells an icon which cell of its atlas to show, as four numbers for CSS: its column and row, and the atlas's
  columns and rows. Scripts give CSS only numbers.
- compose: Writes a text with placeholders into an element, putting elements such as the source link where their
  placeholder is. Lets a translated sentence contain a link.
- demand: Checks that a controller has every action a view needs, and stops with an error that names the missing ones.
  treeview.js and optionsview.js call it as they start, so a missing action shows at once.
- writeFixed: Writes the texts that never change: the report window's English words, the source's, Microsoft's and the
  project's links, and Microsoft's notice around its links. Runs once at the start.
- writeTexts: Writes every text of the chosen language: the page's `lang`, each element marked `data-text`, and the
  footer's source and date (Req. 5, 11).
- textsView: The view of the texts: writes them again at the start and when the language changes.
- share: Notes the names that more than one row uses, so that Filters can say which one is meant.
- chipName: Names an entry of Filters from what chipOf() gives: a shared name "in" its parent, and "Full" before a
  building or group marked in full (Req. 24).
- civIcon: Makes a civilisation's icon from its template, showing its cell of the civilisations' atlas. Only used
  without Simple UI.
- listCivs: Lists the civilisations that fit the marks (matchingCivs()), translated and sorted (Req. 4), with icons
  and links to aoe2techtree.net without Simple UI; writes the count, and the hint when the list is empty.
- civsView: The view of the civilisations: lists them again after every change.
- listFilters: Makes one Filters button per entry (Req. 22, 23), with its mark and name, and a label telling screen
  readers what a click clears. Hides Filters when nothing is marked.
- filtersView: The view of Filters: notes the shared names again when the tree or the language changed, then makes the
  buttons again.
- showPage: Shows the header and the content, which stay hidden until the page has started.
- showLoadError: Shows, or hides, the message that the data or a language could not be loaded.
- spin: Gives the loading picture its address, kept in `data-src` so that it loads only when the loading screen shows
  it.
- showLoading: Shows or hides the loading screen, with its turning picture, made once, and makes the page behind it
  unreachable while it shows.

@ js/view/treeview.js
The tree on the left as the visitor sees it: its rows with their names, boxes, icons and counts, which branches are
  open, what Find found, and the focus. It is a part of the page in page.js, and hands every click, touch and key to
  the actions of nodetree.js, its controller (Strategy).
- rowOf: The elements of an item's row: its entry, row, name, "must lack" words, counts, arrow, list and icon. They are
  kept here, by item, so that the model's items hold no element (Test 85).
- opened: Tells whether a row's branch is open, read from its arrow's `aria-expanded`, which is the one place this is
  kept.
- isOpen: Tells whether an item's branch is open.
- shown: Tells whether an item's row can be seen, that is, whether every branch above it is open.
- heading: Tells whether a group is only a heading inside an item, which has no box and cannot be marked.
- focusOf: The part of a row that takes focus: a heading's arrow, or any other row's name.
- itemAt: Returns the tree item an element belongs to, but not a heading, so one listener on the tree serves every row,
  and the tooltip knows when it is over the tree.
- dress: Gives a tree row its icon between its box and name, from its cell of its atlas, with its kind and reach for the
  outline, or takes it away with Simple UI on (Rules 9, 22).
- build: Makes a row for each item from the template, with its arrow, and ids that tie the arrow and the list to the
  name for screen readers. A heading loses its box. Calls itself for the items below.
- endPress: Cancels a long press in progress and its highlight, when the finger moves or lifts, or the tree is drawn
  again, and notes when a touch ended.
- replant: Draws the tree from the model again, keeping the branches that were open open by their paths, and dresses
  each row. Runs at the start and whenever the tree is built again.
- label: Writes every row's name in the current language.
- count: Writes an item's count of civilisations that fit the marks, from the model's countOf(): its own number, or
  "own–all" when it is closed with items under it, and a spoken version for screen readers.
- setOpen: Opens or closes an item's branch, and rewrites its count, since a closed item shows two numbers.
- paint: Updates every box: its state for the eye, `aria-checked` for screen readers, the hidden "must lack" words, the
  bold of a row's own mark, and greyed-out rows and arrows.
- treeView: The view of the tree: draws it again when the tree was built again, dresses its rows when Simple UI
  changes, names them when the language changes, then closes greyed-out items, paints the boxes and writes every
  count. Then it hands the change to its controller's redrawn(), so that Find follows the tree as it is drawn now.
- focus: Moves focus to an item's row, or to its arrow when asked and it has one.
- highlight: Highlights the items Find found, and announces how many there are, or that there are none (Req. 20, 21).
- reveal: Scrolls the tree's own list just enough to show a row, and nothing around it, so the Find box being typed in
  never moves (Rule 18).
- findText: What is typed in the Find box.
- onKey: Hands a key in the tree to the controller's key(), with Shift, the other modifier keys and whether an arrow
  has focus, and stops the browser's own action when the controller used the key (WCAG 2.1.1).
- onPress: Starts a right-click, or a touch that becomes a long press after half a second, which marks must lack (Req.
  3). The row shows as pressed when the long press is ready.
- onRelease: Ends a press: reports a hold on the item to the controller if the press was ready and ends on the same
  item, and swallows the click that follows.
- onMenu: Stops the browser's own menu over the tree, and reports the menu key to the controller. Right-clicks and long
  presses are left to onPress() and onRelease().
- onClick: Opens or closes a branch from its arrow, or a heading from its name, or reports a click on an item to the
  controller, unless the click ends a long press. What a gesture marks is the controller's to decide.
- onChip: Has the controller clear the marks behind a Filters button, and moves focus to the next button, or to the
  heading after the last one (WCAG 2.4.3).
- wireTools: Connects Expand all and Collapse all, which hand focus to each other without Simple UI, Clear (Req. 17 to
  19), Find with its clearing cross and Enter, and the Filters buttons, each to its action. Runs once at the start.
- startTreeView: Starts the tree's view with its controller's actions, checked with demand(), and connects keys,
  presses, moves that cancel a long press, the menu and clicks, then the tools around the tree. Runs once, from
  startTree().

@ js/view/optionsview.js
The Options window as the visitor sees it: the language menu, the boxes and Civ view, as they are ticked and picked
  before OK. It is a part of the page in page.js, and hands opening the window and OK to the actions of
  optionshandler.js, its controller (Strategy).
- options: Lists the language menu's buttons. Used by the menu's keys and by the tests.
- setMenu: Opens or closes the language menu, and moves focus if asked. With Simple UI it hides the settings while the
  list is open, so that the window keeps its size (Rule 18); without Simple UI the list lies over them, as the game's
  combo box does.
- buildLanguages: Makes one button per language, named and tagged in its own language, so screen readers say each name
  right (WCAG 3.1.2). Runs once at the start.
- showLanguage: Shows a language as chosen in the window: its name on the menu's button and its mark in the list. OK
  makes it the page's language.
- tick: Shows an Options box as ticked or not, for the eye and for screen readers.
- showView: Marks the chosen view among the Civ view radio buttons.
- picked: The language marked in the window's list, which OK makes the page's language.
- checked: Tells whether an Options box is ticked.
- toggle: Ticks or unticks an Options box in the window only; OK applies it.
- readers: How the window reads the two settings that are no boxes: the language and Civ view.
- shown: What the window shows for a setting: its own reading, or its box.
- show: Shows a value of a setting in the window: the language, Civ view, or a box.
- optionsView: The view of the Options window: marks the page's language in it at the start and when the language
  changes.
- wireOptions: Connects the cogwheel button, which has the controller show the page's values in the window before it
  opens, OK, which has the controller apply the window, and the boxes, by click and by Space. Runs once at the start.
- onMenuKey: Handles the language menu's keys: Down and Up open it at the current language and move through it, Home and
  End jump, and Escape closes it and returns focus (WCAG 2.1.1).
- onScrollbar: Tells whether a click landed on the language list's own scroll bar, which Chrome reports as a click, so
  that scrolling the list does not close it.
- wireLanguages: Connects the language menu: its button, a click on a language or anywhere else in the list (Req. 11,
  12), its keys, and closing when focus or a click goes elsewhere. Runs once at the start.
- startOptionsView: Starts the Options window's view with its controller's actions, checked with demand(), and
  connects the language menu and the window. Runs once, from startOptions().

@ js/view/page.js
The whole page as one view (Composite), and the model's only observer (Observer). It holds the views of the texts,
  Options, the tree, the civilisations and Filters, and passes each change to all of them, always in that order. Its
  update, draw(), is only for the model's notices; redraw() draws the page for the start and for Simple UI.
- draw: Passes a change to every view, in order: the model's `"marks"`, `"tree"` and `"language"`, `"start"` once at
  the start, and `"look"` after Simple UI changes.
- redraw: Draws the page again without a change of the model: `"start"` once, from start(), and `"look"` when Simple
  UI changes, from applySimple().
- startPage: Has the model tell the page of its changes. Runs once, from start().

@ js/view/scrollbars.js
The game's scroll bars, which replace the browser's without Simple UI. A bar is placed after each list that scrolls, so
  it does not scroll with it, and CSS draws it from the numbers fit() gives it.
- fit: Gives a list's bar its place along the list's right edge, its height and the knob's position, as numbers for CSS,
  and hides the bar with Simple UI or when the list holds no more than it shows, and the knob when the bar is too short
  for it.
- fitAll: Fits every bar again. Runs when a list or what is in it changes size, when anything is added or taken out,
  when Simple UI changes and when the window does.
- watch: Has the size observer watch a list and everything directly in it, so a bar is fitted again when they change
  size.
- hold: Makes an arrow or the bar's body act on a press, and again every 50 milliseconds after 400 while held, keeping
  hold of the pointer so the press stays while it leaves.
- page: Scrolls a list by a page, less a line, towards a press above or below the knob, and not at all once the knob
  reaches the pointer.
- drag: Lets the knob be dragged: the list scrolls as far as the knob moves, scaled from the body's room to the list's.
- wire: Connects a bar: the arrows scroll a line, the body pages, the knob drags and the wheel over the bar scrolls the
  list; the list's scrolling fits the bar again. A line is 40 pixels, but in the language list without Simple UI it is
  one language, and the wheel there, over the list or its bar, scrolls a language at a time, so that the list always
  shows whole languages.
- startBars: Puts a bar from its template after every list that scrolls, connects it, and fits the bars again whenever
  anything about them changes. Runs once, from startPictures().

@ js/view/pictures.js
What only the pictures need: Civ view, list or tiles, the tooltip, and the pictures and loading screen that come with
  leaving Simple UI. It also starts the scroll bars.
- setView: Shows the civilisations as a list or as tiles, and marks the choice among the radio buttons. Tiles are
  pictures, so CSS shows them only without Simple UI.
- setTips: Switches the tooltips on or off, and ticks the Tooltips box.
- branchTip: The tooltip of a branch's arrow, or of the name of a group inside a building, which has no box and opens on
  a click: to collapse the branch while it is open, to expand it while it is closed, and none on a locked branch, whose
  arrow is disabled.
- tipFor: Returns the tooltip's text for what the mouse is over: the marking hint over the tree, the text that an
  element's `data-tip` names over every control, the hint of a branch's arrow (branchTip()), or the address of a
  civilisation's or the footer's link.
- wireTip: Moves the tooltip with the mouse, with its corner at the cursor picture's corner and inside the window, and
  puts it into the Options window while that is open. After every click it works the tooltip out again for what is under
  the mouse (`elementFromPoint`), so that it follows a button that changes, such as Expand all turning into Collapse
  all. It shows only for a mouse, without Simple UI and with Tooltips on.
- screenPictures: Works out the pictures for the screen from its size in device pixels: the parchment (`fhd`, `qhd`,
  `uhd` or `ultrawide`, and `fhd` on a phone) and the size of every other picture, `sd` or `4k`.
- rulesIn: Lists every rule of a style sheet, with the rules inside media queries.
- linksIn: Lists the picture and font addresses in a rule's text, made whole from the style sheet's own address.
- filesOf: Lists the addresses in a style sheet's rules whose selectors pass a test.
- rooted: Tells whether a selector already holds for `<html>` or `<body>`, as for the parchment and the cursors, whose
  pictures show at once.
- fits: Tells whether a picture is the one for the screen: the parchment of `data-background`, and the size of
  `data-assets`. Pictures without a size always fit.
- pictureFiles: Lists the pictures and fonts in the style sheets that the screen uses, so the loading screen can wait
  for them all.
- copy: Loads a picture once into a kept image, so that the browser has it before the page shows it.
- onPage: The addresses of the images already in the page, but for the loading screen's own.
- done: Waits for a kept image to be decoded, or to fail, so that waiting never stops.
- ready: Tells whether every picture and font has loaded already.
- finishLoading: Hides the loading screen once every picture and font has loaded, at once if they have already. Without
  Simple UI only; a later call takes over from an earlier one.
- applySimple: The page's part of switching Simple UI (Req. 32, 33): the first time it picks the screen's pictures and
  sets `--pixel`, the size of one screen pixel; it shows the loading screen when leaving Simple UI, adds or removes the
  `pictures` class, and has redraw() draw the page's views again with `"look"`.
- startPictures: Starts the tooltip and the scroll bars. Runs once, after start().

@ js/view/report.js
The report window of the updater and the tests. It only shows what they found.
- openReport: Opens the report window, used by the tests and the updater, with a title and a status line, and empties
  it.
- report: Adds a line to the report window.
- showUpdating: Opens the report window for the updater, saying that it is loading the source's data.
- showUpdate: Shows what the updater found: each change, the counts of civilisations, items and languages, and each
  changed file to download; or that the update is blocked, when the new data cannot be used.
- showUpdateFailed: Shows why the update failed.

@ js/controller/nodetree.js
What the visitor's clicks, touches and keys in the tree mean: marking, opening and closing, the arrow keys, Find, the
  buttons above the tree and the buttons of Filters. These are the actions treeview.js is started with (Strategy).
  They change the model, or ask the tree's view to open a branch or move the focus, and never touch the page. The view
  hands them each change of the model once it has drawn it, so they never subscribe to the model.
- expand: Opens an item and each only child below it, so opening Militia shows its whole line in one step, unless the
  item is greyed out and closed. Used by the arrows and the Right key.
- branch: Opens or closes a branch, from its arrow or a heading's name.
- toggle: Handles a mark from a gesture or a key, unless the item is greyed out: opens the way to every row the mark
  will reach (reach(), Req. 28, 31), then calls mark().
- gesture: Turns what the tree's view reports into a mark: a click marks must have (Req. 3), and a long press, a
  right-click and the menu key must lack, as key() decides for Space and X.
- search: Notes the items whose names hold Find's text, but those inside closed greyed-out items (behind()), and has
  the view highlight them and say how many there are (Req. 20, 21).
- find: Runs Find: searches, opens the branches down to the matches and shows the first (Req. 20, 21).
- redrawn: Keeps Find up to date once the tree's view has drawn a change: after marks it only searches again, so that
  no branch opens or closes, and after a new tree, a new language or the start it runs the whole of Find again, since
  the names or the rows changed. Nothing changes for `"look"`.
- enter: Opens the branches down to the first item Find found and moves focus to it, on Enter in its box.
- key: Handles a key in the tree: Space marks must have and X must lack (Req. 3); Up, Down, Home and End move; Right
  and Left open, enter, leave and close branches (WCAG 2.1.1). Tells the view whether it used the key.
- expandAll: Opens every branch but those of closed greyed-out items.
- collapseAll: Closes every branch.
- startTree: Starts the tree's view with these actions, and with the model's clear() and unmarkLine() for Clear and the
  Filters buttons. Runs once, from start().

@ js/controller/optionshandler.js
What the settings of the Options window do: the language, the two Include boxes, Simple UI, Civ view and Tooltips, each
  saved and applied on OK. Its reset() and apply() are the actions optionsview.js is started with (Strategy). The
  language in use is the model's alone: only the one still loading is kept here, as `pending`.
- setLanguage: Makes a language the page's through the model's chooseLanguage(), which tells the page. Runs when a
  language is chosen and its file is loaded.
- setInclude: Switches Include Unique and Universal (Req. 13 to 15): has the model build the tree again, keeping marks,
  saves the choice and ticks the box.
- setRegional: Switches Include Regionals: has the model build the tree again, keeping marks, saves the choice and
  ticks the box.
- savedSimple: Reads the saved Simple UI choice: off unless it was saved as on, so a first visit shows the pictures.
- setSimple: Switches Simple UI (Req. 32, 33): saves the choice, ticks the box and has applySimple() change the page.
  Simple UI is only how the page looks, so it does not go through the model.
- chooseView: Chooses Civ view, list or tiles: saves the choice and shows it.
- chooseTips: Switches Tooltips on or off: saves the choice and shows it.
- choices: Every setting of the Options window as an entry: how to read its value now and how to set it. reset() and
  apply() go through it.
- apply: Applies every setting whose window value differs from the page's, when OK is pressed.
- reset: Shows the page's values again in the window, dropping what was changed, each time the window opens.
- choose: Chooses a language: loads its file if needed, as `pending` so that only the last one chosen is used, shows
  it, saves the choice, and has keepInAddress() put it in the address (`?lng=`), so a shared link keeps it. If the
  file cannot be loaded, it shows the load error and puts the window back on the language in use.
- startOptions: Starts the Options window: ticks the two Include boxes as the model has them, starts the window's
  view with reset() and apply(), builds the language menu, and restores Civ view and Tooltips. Runs once, from
  start().

@ js/controller/updatehandler.js
Runs the updater, which `?update` opens.
- runUpdate: Runs the updater: shows that it is loading, has datahandler.js fetch the new data and work out what
  changed, and has report.js show it, or why it failed. It changes nothing by itself.

@ js/tests/run_test.js
Runs the tests and shows the report. Loaded only when the address ends in `?test`.
- merge: Combines the results into one line per test, failed if any of its checks failed, with the first details and the
  window's size. A test may check in several states and languages.
- reportLine: Writes a test's report line, such as "Test 15 failed: ...", from its number, name and details.
- loadFailed: Marks the tests as failed when the page could not load, so CI never waits for a result that cannot come.
- prepare: Notes the visitor's language, options and saved choices, then sets the tests' starting point: Include
  unticked, Include Regionals ticked, Simple UI and Tooltips on.
- restore: Puts back the visitor's language, options, view, address and saved choices after the tests.
- rounds: Runs every test in three rounds, functional, accessibility and maintainability, in the order TESTS.md lists
  them, picking sample items afresh where the tree was rebuilt.
- runTests: Runs the tests, numbers them in the order they first ran, checks the 100-test limit (Rule 17), marks the
  page passed or failed for CI, and shows the report, failures first. Its Close leaves the tests: it opens the page's
  own address, without `?test`.
