@ js/view/stringbuilder.js
The page's text: the fixed texts, the texts of the chosen language, the tree's names, the list of civilisations and
  Filters, the loading screen, and the small helpers every script that touches the page uses.
- $: Short for `document.getElementById`, used wherever the page is touched.
- clone: Copies the element inside a template in index.html. Repeated markup lives in index.html (Rule 2); the code only
  fills it in.
- simple: Tells whether Simple UI is on, from the `pictures` class on the page, which is what shows the pictures. One
  source of truth, readable from every script.
- place: Tells an icon which cell of its atlas to show, as four numbers for CSS: its column and row, and the atlas's
  columns and rows. Scripts give CSS only numbers.
- compose: Writes a text with placeholders into an element, putting elements such as the source link where their
  placeholder is. Lets a translated sentence contain a link.
- writeFixed: Writes the texts that never change: the report window's English words, the source's, Microsoft's and the
  project's links, and Microsoft's notice around its links. Runs once at the start.
- writeTexts: Writes every text of the chosen language: the page's `lang`, each element marked `data-text`, and the
  footer's source and date (Req. 5, 11).
- label: Writes every tree item's name in the current language, and notes the names used more than once.
- civIcon: Makes a civilisation's icon from its template, showing its cell of the civilisations' atlas. Only used
  without Simple UI.
- listCivs: Lists the civilisations that fit the marks, translated and sorted (Req. 4), with icons and links to
  aoe2techtree.net without Simple UI; writes the count, and the hint when the list is empty.
- listFilters: Makes one Filters button per entry (Req. 22, 23), with its mark and name, and a label telling screen
  readers what a click clears. Hides Filters when nothing is marked.
- showLoadError: Shows, or hides, the message that the data or a language could not be loaded.
- spin: Gives the loading picture its address, kept in `data-src` so that it loads only when the loading screen shows
  it.
- showLoading: Shows or hides the loading screen, with its turning picture, made once, and makes the page behind it
  unreachable while it shows.

@ js/view/pictures.js
What only the pictures need: Civ view, list or tiles, and the tooltip. It also starts the scroll bars.
- showView: Marks the chosen view among the Civ view radio buttons.
- setView: Shows the civilisations as a list or as tiles, remembers the choice and marks it among the radio buttons.
  Tiles are pictures, so CSS shows them only without Simple UI.
- setTips: Switches the tooltips on or off, remembers the choice, and ticks the Tooltips box.
- branchTip: The tooltip of a branch's arrow, or of the name of a group inside a building, which has no box and opens on
  a click: to collapse the branch while it is open, to expand it while it is closed, and none on a locked branch, whose
  arrow is disabled.
- tipFor: Returns the tooltip's text for what the mouse is over: the marking hint over the tree, the text that an
  element's `data-tip` names over every control, the hint of a branch's arrow (branchTip()), or the address of a
  civilisation's or the footer's link.
- wireTip: Moves the tooltip with the mouse, with its corner at the cursor picture's corner and inside the window, and
  puts it into the Options window while that is open. After every click it works the tooltip out again for what is under
  the mouse (`elementFromPoint`), so that it follows a button that changes, such as Expand all turning into Collapse
  all. It shows only for a mouse, without Simple UI and with Tooltips on.
- startPictures: Restores Civ view and Tooltips from the saved choices, adds both to the Options window's choices,
  starts the tooltip and the scroll bars. Runs once, after start().

@ js/view/scrollbars.js
The game's scroll bars, which replace the browser's without Simple UI. A bar is placed after each list that scrolls, so
  it does not scroll with it, and CSS draws it from the numbers fit() gives it.
- fit: Gives a list's bar its place along the list's right edge, its height and the knob's position, as numbers for CSS,
  and hides the bar with Simple UI or when the list holds no more than it shows, and the knob when the bar is too short
  for it.
- fitAll: Fits every bar again. Runs when a list or what is in it changes size, when anything is added or taken out,
  when Simple UI changes and when the window does.
- watch: Has the size observer watch a list and everything directly in it, so a bar is fitted again when they change
  size.
- hold: Makes an arrow or the bar's body act on a press, and again every 50 milliseconds after 400 while held, keeping
  hold of the pointer so the press stays while it leaves.
- page: Scrolls a list by a page, less a line, towards a press above or below the knob, and not at all once the knob
  reaches the pointer.
- drag: Lets the knob be dragged: the list scrolls as far as the knob moves, scaled from the body's room to the list's.
- wire: Connects a bar: the arrows scroll a line, the body pages, the knob drags and the wheel over the bar scrolls the
  list; the list's scrolling fits the bar again. A line is 40 pixels, but in the language list without Simple UI it is
  one language, and the wheel there, over the list or its bar, scrolls a language at a time, so that the list always
  shows whole languages.
- startBars: Puts a bar from its template after every list that scrolls, connects it, and fits the bars again whenever
  anything about them changes. Runs once, from startPictures().

@ js/view/report.js
The report window of the updater and the tests.
- openReport: Opens the report window, used by the tests and the updater, with a title and a status line, and empties
  it.
- report: Adds a line to the report window.
- runUpdate: Runs the updater: shows its progress, lists what changed, and offers each changed file for download. Opened
  with `?update`; it changes nothing by itself.

@ js/controller/nodetree.js
The tree on the left: drawing its rows, opening and closing them, their counts, marking with clicks, touch and keys,
  Find, and the buttons above the tree.
- isOpen: Tells whether an item's branch is open, read from its arrow's `aria-expanded`, which is the one place this is
  kept.
- shown: Tells whether an item's row can be seen, that is, whether every branch above it is open.
- behind: Tells whether an item lies inside a closed greyed-out item, where Find does not look.
- heading: Tells whether a group is only a heading inside an item, which has no box and cannot be marked.
- focusOf: The part of a row that takes focus: a heading's arrow, or any other row's name.
- itemAt: Returns the tree item an element belongs to, but not a heading, so one listener on the tree serves every row,
  and the tooltip knows when it is over the tree.
- dress: Gives a tree row its icon between its box and name, from its cell of its atlas, with its kind and reach for the
  outline, or takes it away with Simple UI on (Rules 9, 22).
- build: Makes a row for each item from the template, with its arrow, and ids that tie the arrow and the list to the
  name for screen readers. A heading loses its box. Calls itself for the items below.
- endPress: Cancels a long press in progress and its highlight, when the finger moves or lifts, or the tree is drawn
  again, and notes when a touch ended.
- plant: Draws the tree from the model again, keeping open branches open, and dresses each row. Runs at the start and
  through replant().
- count: Writes an item's count of civilisations that fit the marks: its own number, or "own–all" when it is closed with
  items under it, and a spoken version for screen readers.
- setOpen: Opens or closes an item's branch, and rewrites its count, since a closed item shows two numbers.
- expand: Opens an item and each only child below it, so opening Militia shows its whole line in one step, unless the
  item is greyed out and closed. Used by the arrows and the Right key.
- paint: Updates every box: its state for the eye, `aria-checked` for screen readers, the hidden "must lack" words, the
  bold of a row's own mark, and greyed-out rows and arrows.
- update: Redraws all that depends on the marks: closes greyed-out items, then the boxes, the civilisation list and its
  count, every item's count and Filters. Runs after every change.
- toggle: Handles a mark from a click, a key, a right-click or a long press, unless the item is greyed out: calls
  mark(), opens the way to every row it reached (Req. 28, 31), and redraws.
- reveal: Scrolls the tree's own list just enough to show a row, and nothing around it, so the Find box being typed in
  never moves (Rule 18).
- find: Runs Find: highlights the matching items, announces how many there are, opens the branches down to them and
  shows the first (Req. 20, 21).
- replant: Builds the tree again and redraws it, its names and its counts, and runs Find again if it holds text. Used
  when an Include box changes.
- onKey: Handles keys in the tree: Space marks must have and X must lack (Req. 3); Up, Down, Home and End move; Right
  and Left open, enter, leave and close branches (WCAG 2.1.1).
- onPress: Starts a right-click, or a touch that becomes a long press after half a second, which marks must lack (Req.
  3). The row shows as pressed when the long press is ready.
- onRelease: Ends a press: marks the item must lack if the press was ready and ends on the same item, and swallows the
  click that follows.
- onMenu: Stops the browser's own menu over the tree, and marks must lack on the menu key. Right-clicks and long presses
  are left to onPress() and onRelease().
- onClick: Opens or closes a branch from its arrow, or a heading from its name, or marks an item must have (Req. 3),
  unless the click ends a long press.
- onChip: Clears the marks behind a Filters button, and moves focus to the next button, or to the heading after the last
  one (WCAG 2.4.3).
- wireTools: Connects Expand all and Collapse all, which hand focus to each other without Simple UI, Clear (Req. 17 to
  19), Find with its clearing cross and Enter, and the Filters buttons. Runs once at the start.
- startTree: Draws the tree and connects it: keys, presses, moves that cancel a long press, the menu and clicks, then
  the tools around it. Runs once at the start.

@ js/controller/optionshandler.js
The Options window and the choices in it: the language menu, the two Include boxes and Simple UI, with the screen's
  pictures, the loading screen and the tab icon. It also starts the page with the visitor's saved choices.
- options: Lists the language menu's buttons. Used by the menu's keys and by the tests.
- setMenu: Opens or closes the language menu, and moves focus if asked. With Simple UI it hides the settings while the
  list is open, so that the window keeps its size (Rule 18); without Simple UI the list lies over them, as the game's
  combo box does.
- buildLanguages: Makes one button per language, named and tagged in its own language, so screen readers say each name
  right (WCAG 3.1.2). Runs once at the start.
- showLanguage: Shows a language as chosen in the window: its name on the menu's button and its mark in the list. OK
  makes it the page's language.
- setLanguage: Shows the page in a language: its texts, the menu's name and mark, the tree's names, then redraws, and
  runs Find again if it holds text. Runs at the start and when a language is chosen.
- tick: Shows an Options box as ticked or not, for the eye and for screen readers.
- setInclude: Switches Include Unique and Universal (Req. 13 to 15): saves the choice, ticks the box and builds the tree
  again, keeping marks.
- setRegional: Switches Include Regionals: saves the choice, ticks the box and builds the tree again, keeping marks.
- showIcon: Gives the tab its icon from data.json without Simple UI, and the empty icon `data:,` with it, so Simple UI
  uses no image at all (Req. 33).
- savedSimple: Reads the saved Simple UI choice: off unless it was saved as on, so a first visit shows the pictures.
- screenPictures: Works out the pictures for the screen from its size in device pixels: the parchment (`fhd`, `qhd`,
  `uhd` or `ultrawide`, and `fhd` on a phone) and the size of every other picture, `sd` or `4k`.
- rulesIn: Lists every rule of a style sheet, with the rules inside media queries.
- linksIn: Lists the picture and font addresses in a rule's text, made whole from the style sheet's own address.
- filesOf: Lists the addresses in a style sheet's rules whose selectors pass a test.
- rooted: Tells whether a selector already holds for `<html>` or `<body>`, as for the parchment and the cursors, whose
  pictures show at once.
- fits: Tells whether a picture is the one for the screen: the parchment of `data-background`, and the size of
  `data-assets`. Pictures without a size always fit.
- pictureFiles: Lists the pictures and fonts in the style sheets that the screen uses, so the loading screen can wait
  for them all.
- copy: Loads a picture once into a kept image, so that the browser has it before the page shows it.
- onPage: The addresses of the images already in the page, but for the loading screen's own.
- done: Waits for a kept image to be decoded, or to fail, so that waiting never stops.
- ready: Tells whether every picture and font has loaded already.
- finishLoading: Hides the loading screen once every picture and font has loaded, at once if they have already. Without
  Simple UI only; a later call takes over from an earlier one.
- setSimple: Switches Simple UI (Req. 32, 33): saves the choice, ticks the box, the first time picks the screen's
  pictures and sets `--pixel`, the size of one screen pixel, shows the loading screen when leaving Simple UI, adds or
  removes the `pictures` class, sets the tab icon, and dresses the tree's rows and the list again.
- picked: The language marked in the window's list, which OK makes the page's language.
- choices: Every setting of the Options window as an entry: how to read its value now, set it, and for the language and
  Civ view read and show what the window shows. startPictures() adds Civ view and Tooltips. Cancel and OK go through it.
- checked: Tells whether an Options box is ticked.
- shownOf: What the window shows for a setting: its own reading, or its box.
- toggle: Ticks or unticks an Options box in the window only; OK applies it.
- apply: Applies every setting whose window value differs from the page's, when OK is pressed.
- discard: Shows the page's values again in the window, dropping what was changed, each time the window opens.
- wireOptions: Connects the cogwheel button, the Options window, OK and its boxes, by click and by Space. Runs once at
  the start.
- choose: Chooses a language: loads its file if needed, shows it, saves the choice, and puts it in the address
  (`?lng=`), so a shared link keeps it. Shows the load error if the file cannot be loaded.
- onMenuKey: Handles the language menu's keys: Down and Up open it at the current language and move through it, Home and
  End jump, and Escape closes it and returns focus (WCAG 2.1.1).
- onScrollbar: Tells whether a click landed on the language list's own scroll bar, which Chrome reports as a click, so
  that scrolling the list does not close it.
- wireLanguages: Connects the language menu: its button, a click on a language or anywhere else in the list (Req. 11,
  12), its keys, and closing when focus or a click goes elsewhere. Runs once at the start.
- start: Starts the page: restores the Include choices, draws the tree, builds the language menu, writes the fixed texts
  and the tab icon, connects every control, shows the chosen language, and reveals the page.

@ js/tests/run_test.js
Runs the tests and shows the report. Loaded only when the address ends in `?test`.
- merge: Combines the results into one line per test, failed if any of its checks failed, with the first details and the
  window's size. A test may check in several states and languages.
- reportLine: Writes a test's report line, such as "Test 15 failed: ...", from its number, name and details.
- loadFailed: Marks the tests as failed when the page could not load, so CI never waits for a result that cannot come.
- prepare: Notes the visitor's language, options and saved choices, then sets the tests' starting point: Include
  unticked, Include Regionals ticked, Simple UI and Tooltips on.
- restore: Puts back the visitor's language, options, view, address and saved choices after the tests.
- rounds: Runs every test in three rounds, functional, accessibility and maintainability, in the order TESTS.md lists
  them, picking sample items afresh where the tree was rebuilt.
- runTests: Runs the tests, numbers them in the order they first ran, checks the 100-test limit (Rule 17), marks the
  page passed or failed for CI, and shows the report, failures first. Its Close leaves the tests: it opens the page's
  own address, without `?test`.
